Click a Question to See the Why Behind It
"What's going on for you right now that made you reach out today?" Motivation ▼
This surfaces urgency and emotional driver. Are they unemployed, underpaid, career-switching, or just curious? Their answer shapes everything else.
"Have you looked into any other programs or schools before calling us?" Competitive ▼
Tells you if they're actively comparing options. If yes, you can differentiate on placement rate, program length, and certifications. If no, they're early in the process.
"What does a successful outcome look like for you — what are you hoping your life looks like after you complete the program?" Vision ▼
Gets them talking about their future, not just their situation. Helps you connect the program to their actual goal — whether that's income, independence, stability, or something else.
"Do you have any flexibility in your schedule right now, or are you working around a job or family commitments?" Logistics ▼
Surfaces schedule constraints early. If they're working full-time or have kids, you can talk about part-time options before they bring it up as an objection.
"Is cost something that's on your mind, or have you thought about how you'd fund the program?" Financial ▼
Opens the financial aid door without pressure. If they haven't thought about it, it's a value-add to walk them through options. If they have concerns, better to address them now.
"Is there anything holding you back from moving forward — or does this feel like the right direction?" Readiness ▼
A soft close that also surfaces hidden objections. If they hesitate, you learn what needs to be addressed. If they're ready, this leads naturally into scheduling the visit.

Veteran Benefits Path Active

Stratatech is approved for VA education benefits. Walk through the key points below and make sure veteran benefits are flagged in the student's record before scheduling.

VA Benefits Conversation
Say "First, thank you for your service. The good news is Stratatech is approved to accept VA education benefits — so depending on what you're eligible for, your training could be fully covered or significantly reduced in cost."
Ask "Do you know which GI Bill benefit you have — is it the Post-9/11 GI Bill, the Montgomery GI Bill, or something else? Or have you had a chance to check your eligibility yet?"
Advisor Tip Many veterans don't know which chapter they have or whether they've used any entitlement. Don't assume — ask. If they're unsure, direct them to VA.gov or our veterans services coordinator.
Key Benefits Reference
Post-9/11 (Ch. 33)
Covers tuition, housing allowance (BAH), and a book stipend. Most comprehensive benefit.
Montgomery (Ch. 30)
Monthly stipend paid to student. Student pays tuition first, then receives payment.
VR&E (Ch. 31)
For service-connected disabilities. Can cover full training costs plus living expenses.
MyCAA
For military spouses. Up to $4,000 for career training programs.
Additional Talking Points
Say "We have a dedicated veterans services coordinator on campus who works with students on the paperwork and makes sure your benefits are applied correctly from day one. You won't be navigating that alone."
Say "A lot of our veteran students find that their military training actually lines up really well with our programs — especially in [Welding / Electrical / Auto Tech]. That background gives you a real head start."
Veteran Checklist
  • Veteran status flagged in student record
  • GI Bill chapter identified (or unknown — follow up)
  • Veterans services coordinator referral made or scheduled
  • Benefits eligibility / remaining entitlement discussed
The Ask
Say "Based on everything you've shared, I think the best next step is to get you on campus so you can see the facilities, meet some of the instructors, and really get a feel for whether this is the right place for you. We do campus visits most weekdays and some Saturdays — what does your schedule look like in the next week or two?"
If They Hesitate "There's absolutely no commitment involved — it's just a tour and a conversation. A lot of students tell us the visit was what made it click for them. We can do it in under an hour."
Advisor Tip Give them two specific options rather than an open-ended "when works for you." For example: "Would a Tuesday morning or a Thursday afternoon work better?" Choice framing makes it easier to commit.
